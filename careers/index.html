Packaging Line Worker (General Labour)

We are hiring for Packaging Line Worker (General Labour) full-time position. You will be required to work an 8-10hr/day shift. If you are looking to get hired fast and have previous experience working in packaging or any similar industry,  this could be the ideal job for you.

  • You must be capable of performing a variety of standing, crouching, twisting, and bending during an 8-10 continental hour shift
  • Can lift 50 lbs- this may be occasionally required
  • Excellent hand-eye coordination and attention to detail
  • Experience with repetitive tasks
  • Active listening
  • Teamwork and follow instructions
  • Offloading stock, physical counting, and verification
  • Quality check of finished goods to make sure work is according to the provided sample and the packaging requirement
  • Re-packing stocks in provided cartons and palletizing
  • marking stocks and finished goods pallets
  • Keeping the work area clean after each job.
  • Keeping approved samples of the job for future reference
  • Must be well versed in the handling of equipment’s – training will be provided at the start of the job
  • Clear criminal record
  • Forklift certificate/license will be considered an asset
  • Shift Availability: Must be available for any assigned shift in the future – currently only Day Shift
  • Excellent attendance is required during regular hours, with the ability to work overtime as necessary
  • Should maintain good housekeeping in the warehouse
  • Any experience in these positions will be considered an asset: Warehouse Associate, Assembler, Stocker, Order Picker, retail, General Labour
  • Reliable, personal transportation 
  • Education: high school diploma or GED
